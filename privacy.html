Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what data Twice Cut Publisher ("the app") handles when it is connected to a TikTok account.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic: display name, avatar, open ID) — to show which account is connected.
  • An access token and refresh token issued by TikTok — to upload videos to that account (video.upload, video.publish).

How the data is stored

Tokens and profile info are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file, and are never sent to any third party or server operated by us. We do not use analytics, cookies or advertising trackers.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data about other TikTok users, followers or viewers.
  • We do not sell, share or rent any data.
  • We do not read your messages, comments or private content.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the account stays connected. Revoking access in TikTok (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services) invalidates the tokens; the local file can be deleted by the owner at any time. You can also ask us to delete it via the contact below.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 18.
