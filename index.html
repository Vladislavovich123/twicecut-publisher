Twice Cut Publisher

Twice Cut Publisher is a small desktop tool used by Twice Cut Studio to publish its own original videos to its own TikTok accounts on a fixed schedule.

What it does

  • Our production pipeline creates original animated videos (science "what if" stories hosted by our illustrated character George) and short clips.
  • The owner signs in once with Login Kit and authorizes the app for his own TikTok account.
  • At the scheduled time the tool uploads the finished video with Content Posting API (Direct Post), with its caption, cover frame and the required labels: AI-generated content and, when a video contains a sponsor banner, branded content.
  • It only posts to accounts owned by Twice Cut Studio. It does not read, collect or post anything for other users.
